Omen Life

The Game of Life on a Launchpad Mini. The next generation flickers on the grid before it arrives, so you can change it in time.

Press pads to bring cells to life or kill them. The omen flickers on the off-beats and speeds up through the last beat of each generation. Change a cell before the turn, and the omen changes with it.

Births sound as plucks and deaths as low ghosts. Pitch rises to the right and upward (D dorian).

Top row: play, step, slower, faster, omen mode (flicker / steady / off), auto-reseed, reseed (next seed number), clear. Right column: the rule. Space plays and pauses.
